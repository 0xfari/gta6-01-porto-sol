Porto Sol is a 2D top-down open-world crime game: steal cars, outrun the police, fight the Viper gang, and climb from street courier to king of a sun-soaked tropical coastal city across 5 story missions.
## Run it
```sh
node serve.ts
```
Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move/drive, E to steal cars and interact, F to punch, mouse click to shoot, Esc to pause.
